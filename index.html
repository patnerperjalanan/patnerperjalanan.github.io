FAQ

Everything you need to know before booking.

How do I book?

Click any Book button and send us your travel date, destination and number of travelers on WhatsApp.

When do I pay?

Booking is made first and payment is settled when you arrive.

Are the prices fixed?

The listed prices are package references. Bromo, Ijen and combined-trip pricing can vary depending on the number of travelers. We confirm the final price before the trip.

Where can I be picked up?

Bromo and Tumpak Sewu packages can be arranged from Surabaya, Malang or Probolinggo. Ijen pickup is available from Surabaya or Malang.

Can I request a private trip?

Yes. Tell us your group size and preferred travel style and we will recommend the best arrangement.
